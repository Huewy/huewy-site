What to do

1. Screenshot the review and the reviewer's profile now, before anything changes.

2. Flag it. You tick one reason, with no room to explain, so pick the closest fit.

3. If the flag is declined, use your one appeal: name the policy, quote the line, point to the profile.

4. Check the listing. Google often doesn't tell you.
