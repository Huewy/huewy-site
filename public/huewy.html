Questions, answered

Good to know.

Does Huewy guarantee removal?

No. Huewy helps you make the strongest accurate case to Google, but only Google decides what violates its policies.

Is this a review management tool?

Not quite. Huewy is focused on the moments when a review crosses the line: evidence, argument, submission, and follow-up.

Does Huewy write anything false?

Never. Huewy drafts from the details you provide and keeps you in control of every claim.
